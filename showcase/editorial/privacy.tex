\chapter{Privacy}

Privacy is security of personal and team data against
unnecessary collection. Shrink the footprint first.
Encryption and network tools then reduce what is left.

Leaks feed phishing, physical targeting, and
reconnaissance. A founder photo from a conference plus
an on-chain label is enough to start a wrench story.

\section{Insights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Active trail and passive trail are both real.
  Posts, WHOIS, data brokers, old Git commits.
  \item A VPN is not anonymity. It hides your path from
  the cafe. It does not hide you from the site you log
  into.
  \item Encrypted chat still has metadata. The member
  list is often the prize.
  \item Product claims change. Verify a tool before you
  bet a key on it.
\end{itemize}

\section{Quick wins}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Search your name and the project. Remove what
  you can from brokers.
  \item HTTPS everywhere. Do not type seeds or keys on
  public Wi-Fi, VPN or not.
  \item Separate identities for public posting and for
  signing.
  \item Encrypted messenger with a recovery story you
  actually tested.
\end{enumerate}
