\chapter{Wallet security}

Wallet security is control of keys and verification of
every signature. Hardware and multisig fail if you sign
attacker-controlled calldata because a website told you
to.

Never sign blindly. The device screen is the
transaction.

This chapter is the 101. The Wallet Security companion
is the book you hand to the person who signs.

\section{Insights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Custody is who can spend. An exchange login is
  still a custodian.
  \item Hot is for spend. Cold is for savings. Mixing
  them is how a drain becomes a life event.
  \item The seed is the wallet. A photo of it is a
  copy of every account it will ever derive.
  \item Approvals are spending permission, not a login.
\end{itemize}

\section{Quick wins}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Know who can spend, including the backup holder
  you forgot.
  \item Buy hardware direct. Reject a device that
  arrives already set up.
  \item Write the seed offline. Never type it into a
  computer.
  \item Official URL, matching address, matching amount,
  hardware screen agrees. Otherwise reject.
\end{enumerate}
