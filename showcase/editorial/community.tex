\chapter{Community}

Community channels are high-trust broadcast paths. A
compromised operator account turns that trust into
phishing at scale. The fake claim link in Discord has
stolen more user funds than most novel contract bugs.

Users believe the official server. That is the point.
Harden the people who can post there.

\section{Insights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Admin roles are keys. Treat them like keys.
  \item Bots and OAuth apps are extra admins with worse
  memory.
  \item Telegram recovery is a phone number unless you
  add two-step verification.
  \item Federation and invite links copy rooms to
  machines you do not control.
\end{itemize}

\section{Quick wins}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item MFA on every official account. No SMS if you can
  avoid it.
  \item Two people to post announcements. One
  compromised intern should not drain the users.
  \item Pin the only official URLs. Tell people search
  results lie.
  \item Revoke stale OAuth and old admin sessions this
  week.
\end{enumerate}
