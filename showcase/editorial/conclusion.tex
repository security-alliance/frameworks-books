\chapter{Conclusion}

The frameworks are one place to put the controls that
keep getting rediscovered after a loss. This book is
the map. The site is the living document. The dedicated
companions are the deep cuts.

You will not get this perfect. The job is to make a
blind signature expensive, a hijacked admin noisy, and
a coerced signer insufficient. Keep the authority small.
Keep the humans alive.
