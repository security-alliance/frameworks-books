\chapter{Treasury}

Treat custodial treasury accounts like cash vaults.
Classify by impact and how fast you need the funds.
Document who can move them. Verify every large transfer
before it is irreversible.

This is for organizations that hold significant value
through a custodian or co-managed setup. Day-to-day
protocol governance is the multisig book.

\section{Insights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Classification sets the control bar. A critical
  account without a delay is a labeled vault with the
  door open.
  \item Registration is how you notice a silent
  permission change.
  \item Address poisoning and rushed wires work because
  people copy from chat.
  \item MPC and policy engines help. They do not replace
  a second human who checks the destination.
\end{itemize}

\section{Quick wins}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item List every account, who can approve, and how
  fast it can move.
  \item Whitelist destinations. Delay new ones.
  \item Test transaction, then the real one. Two people
  on a call for first-time recipients.
  \item Never copy an address from explorer history.
\end{enumerate}
