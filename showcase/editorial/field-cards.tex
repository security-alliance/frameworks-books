\chapter{If it is happening now}

These are prompts, not a local emergency plan. Read them
before you need them.

\section{Someone is being coerced}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Life first. Keys later.
  \item Open the Physical Security companion.
  \item Do not coach them to be a hero over the phone.
  \item After they are safe, assume every secret they
  could reach is burned.
\end{enumerate}

\section{About to sign}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Official URL. Not a search result. Not a chat
  link.
  \item Function, destination, and amount match.
  \item Hardware screen agrees. If it does not, reject.
  \item Full Wallet Security companion if this is a
  habit, not a one-off.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Users are being drained}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Pause if you can. Take the front end down if
  that is the vector.
  \item Official channels only. Pin the real URL.
  \item Incident lead, then SEAL 911 if you need a war
  room.
  \item DNS, CDN, and admin keys are suspects until
  proven otherwise.
\end{enumerate}

\section{A hire or dependency feels wrong}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Stop adding access. Do not confront in the same
  channel they control.
  \item DPRK pages for people. Supply chain pages for
  packages.
  \item Rotate what they could have touched.
  \item Legal and sanctions questions are not optional
  if money already moved.
\end{enumerate}
