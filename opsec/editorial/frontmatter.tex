\chapter{About this book}

Hi reader! o/

This mini-book is an adaptation from the travel security
guide from the Security Frameworks, an initiative by the
Security Alliance. The majority of the content was
initially drafted by me and peer reviewed by other
members of The Red Guild and SEAL.

Part of the contents are inspired and based on resources
from CISA GOV, Cornell University, Unchained, Cypherock,
Schneier, The Mac Guys, BlackCloak, Trio, Travel and
Leisure, Washington Post among others that are cited.

\section{Help us improve!}

Suffice to say this is a work in progress and an
experiment! After receiving a lot of positive feedback on
the original guide I originally drafted for frameworks, it
seemed like a handy travel guide was a no brainer.

I acknowledge most of this guide may not be representative
for the majority. Things move fast in crypto security.
Nonetheless, my peers and I deemed it crucial to improve
Operational Security practices for individuals.

{\itshape When someone asks me about what they should do
under a specific situation I always defer to the best
possible answer non-answer: ``Well\ldots{} it all depends
on your threat model''. Now I can save myself 8 words by
handing them this mini-book.}
