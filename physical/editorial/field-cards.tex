\chapter{If it is happening now}

These are prompts, not a local emergency plan. Read them
before you need them.

\section{Active physical threat}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Protect life. De-escalate. Do not instruct
  resistance.
  \item Assume the victim is still watched or still
  coerced.
  \item One crisis channel. One incident commander. Only
  when that call itself is safe.
  \item Move people. Get medical help.
  \item Contain signers, funds, contracts, and
  credentials only if that does not raise the physical
  risk.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Doxxing or surveillance signal}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Family-specific threats, leaked addresses,
  persistent probing, and the same unknown face again
  are escalation, not vibes.
  \item Lower what people can transact. Cut
  high-privilege work.
  \item Change the exposed routine. Tell the contact you
  already named.
  \item Keep evidence. Do not confront or follow anyone.
  \item Reassess home, work, travel, and signers
  together, not as four tickets.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Device or key may be tampered}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Stop using it to sign or recover.
  \item Isolate it. Do not smash the evidence.
  \item Treat secrets on an unlocked device as gone.
  \item Rebuild on trusted hardware with new material.
  \item Migrate only after the replacement actually
  works.
\end{enumerate}

\section{After, when you reset trust}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Support victims and families before you demand a
  debrief.
  \item Never reuse exposed seeds, keys, credentials, or
  devices.
  \item Re-verify signers, thresholds, allowlists,
  recovery, vendors.
  \item Record decisions on a need-to-know list.
  \item Update the risk register, training, travel rules,
  and the drill calendar.
\end{enumerate}
