\chapter{How you join}

Join with a dedicated hardware-wallet address and
prove you own it before anyone relies on you to
sign.

\section{Fresh address}

New index, labeled, for this Safe only. Two
reasons: your wallet then only shows this set's
traffic, and you are less likely to sign the wrong
request. Remember the seed still ties the indexes
together.

\section{Prove ownership}

Sign a message that names you (or your entity),
the set, and the address. Entity affiliation is
fine. The point is accountability, not doxing.

Publish it, or hand it to the admin to file with
the inventory. Other signers verify the signature
against that address before they add you.

Do this on a tool you already trust, from a
bookmark, not from a chat link.

\section{Out of band}

Adding or replacing a signer is not a chat-message
job. Video plus signed message. If the old key is
gone, identity is video, known social account, or
whatever you pre-agreed. Not a Telegram DM from a
new number.

\section{Channels before the first real tx}

Dedicated channel for this set. Signal is the
usual pick. Backup on a different platform.
Emergency sets need paging that actually reaches
people at 3am. Test it before you need it.

Do not click transaction links. Type or bookmark.

\section{Bookmark the real Safe}

Anyone can create a Safe and add you as owner.
It shows up in the dashboard next to the real
one. Attackers pick addresses that match the first
and last four characters of yours.

Navigate from a bookmark. Check the full address.
If a new Safe appears that you did not expect, do
not touch it.
