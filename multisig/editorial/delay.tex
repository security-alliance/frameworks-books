\chapter{Delay}

Build slowness in on purpose. Speed is a risk.
Forced urgency is how attackers push a signing
ceremony past verification.

Without a timelock, once you have threshold
signatures the change lands immediately. There is
no window to notice. Legitimate operations can
wait. If they cannot, they probably belong on the
pause-only set, not the upgrade set.

\section{Tiered delays}

Not every change is an upgrade.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Long (7--14+ days).} Upgrades,
  token changes, anything users should be able to
  exit.
  \item \textbf{Medium (48--72 hours).} Fees,
  oracles, validator sets. Time to review.
  \item \textbf{Short (hours to a day).} Routine
  knobs inside bounds you already enforced
  on-chain.
  \item \textbf{None.} Pause-only. Nothing else.
\end{itemize}

A default of a few days is a reasonable floor for
things that are not emergencies. Even an hour with
someone watching beats zero.

\section{The window is the control}

A timelock with nobody watching is decoration. The
malicious payload just waits, then executes.

Watch queue, execute, and cancel. Alerts must
include decoded details: target, function,
parameters. Not ``something happened.''

At least one watch is run by someone outside the
core team, so an insider cannot mute every
channel. Use more than one channel. If the Slack
is owned, email still rings.

Make the queue visible to the community if you
can. Extra eyeballs are part of the delay.

\section{Simulate the right thing}

When you sign a timelock queue, the simulation
shows the staging event, not the eventual effect.
Inspect the staged payload. Check who can cancel
and who can execute.

After it lands, verify the on-chain result against
what you thought you queued. Local review and
broadcast payload are not guaranteed to be the
same thing.

\section{Emergency bypass}

If you can skip the delay, that path must be
pause-only. Never upgrade. Never move funds.

A smaller veto group can cancel. Bypassing a
timelock, if you allow it at all, should need the
normal quorum plus extra signers, not fewer.
