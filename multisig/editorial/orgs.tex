\textbf{ABOUT -- SECURITY ALLIANCE}

Security Alliance (SEAL) is a nonprofit organization
dedicated to securing the future of cryptocurrency.
Under the leadership of whitehat hacker \textbf{samczsun},
the organization designs security projects and
coordinates across the crypto industry so that useful
practices actually get used.

Learn more at \plainurl{securityalliance.org}
