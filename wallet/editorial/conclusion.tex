\chapter{Conclusion}

Wallet security is not a brand of device. It is knowing
who can spend, keeping the seed off the network, and
reading the screen every time.

The less any one key can move, the less a phish pays.
The less a contract is approved to pull, the less a
drainer finds. The more independent people you put on
the savings, the less one laptop matters.

None of this is a checklist you tick in January. Balances
grow. New dApps appear. Someone starts storing seeds in
photos again. Re-read the threat when the number
changes, when you become a signer, when a new account
type shows up in a wallet prompt.

If something already happened, do not reuse the
compromised path because it is familiar. Move. Rebuild
from material that was never on that screen.

You will not get this perfect. The job is to make a
blind signature expensive, recovery possible, and panic
unnecessary. Keep the spend small. Keep the seed
offline. Sign only what you can explain.
