\textbf{ABOUT -- SECURITY ALLIANCE}

Security Alliance (SEAL) is a nonprofit organization
dedicated to securing the future of cryptocurrency.
Under the leadership of whitehat hacker \textbf{samczsun},
the organization designs security projects and
coordinates across the crypto industry so that useful
practices actually get used.

Learn more at \plainurl{securityalliance.org}

\vspace{2mm}

\textbf{ABOUT -- THE RED GUILD}

A non-profit organization, think-tank-alike, devoted to
do security as a public good within the Ethereum
ecosystem. Its work spans security research, responsible
disclosure, education, open-source tooling, community
awareness and more. It is best known for developing
gamified learning experiences like \textbf{Damn Vulnerable
DeFi}, and their recent platform the \textbf{Phishing Dojo}.

Learn more at \plainurl{theredguild.org}
